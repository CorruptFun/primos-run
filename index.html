HOW TO PLAY

The controls

  • Swipe ← → / arrows — change lanes
  • Swipe ↑ / space — jump the trash
  • Swipe ↓ / down — duck under the neighbour's laundry
  • Tap anywhere — jump. Same as swiping up.

THE ONE THAT GETS YOU

THESE DO NOT GET JUMPED

Checkpoints, the wall, the ICE cruiser. Every one of them is taller than your jump, and always has been. Jumping one is a hit, every single time. Change lanes instead.

What's in the alley

  • Beers are points, and a streak of them pays more.
  • Tacos are fuel. Run out and ICE takes you.
  • Piñata pulls every beer in the alley to you.
  • Chancla — you go straight through whatever you hit.
  • Skateboard takes one crash for you, and jumps higher.

Every hit you take brings ICE a step closer. Nothing gets it off you — you just stop feeding it.

Alley school

Corrupt teaches the whole thing by doing, in about a minute. Take it as many times as you want.
